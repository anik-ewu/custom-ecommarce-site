1. Who we are

{{ shop.legalName }} ("{{ shop.name }}", "we", "us") sells handmade nakshi kantha and jute goods from {{ shop.city }} through this website. We are responsible for the personal information described here. Questions about privacy go to {{ shop.contactEmail }}.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and your password, which we store only as a secure one-way hash.
  • Order and delivery details: the recipient's name, mobile number and delivery address, the items you ordered, prices and order status.
  • Your cart: the pieces you add, including the 15-minute hold on one-of-a-kind items, whether or not you are signed in.
  • Technical information: your IP address and basic request details in our server logs, which we use to keep the site secure (for example to limit repeated sign-in attempts).

We don't collect card numbers: today we only take cash on delivery. If we add card payments, the card details go directly to our payment processor and never reach our servers.

3. Signing in with Google

If you choose "Sign in with Google" (when we offer it), Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us, and only after you agree on Google's own consent screen. We use this information only to create your {{ shop.name }} account and sign you in. We don't ask for access to your contacts, files, calendar or any other Google data.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or let anyone else read it, except where needed to run this service, for security, or when the law requires it. You can remove {{ shop.name }}'s access at any time in your Google account settings.

4. How we use it

  • To take, confirm, deliver and support your orders, including calling you before delivery.
  • To run your account and keep you signed in.
  • To hold one-of-a-kind pieces for you while they're in your cart.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to keep the site secure.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations in Bangladesh.

We don't send marketing messages, and we don't build advertising profiles.

5. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Delivery partners, who receive the recipient's name, mobile number and address to deliver your parcel.
  • Service providers who host our website, database and images, and (if card payments are added) process payments. They may only use it to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, when Bangladeshi law requires it or to protect our customers and artisans from fraud.

Some of these providers store data on servers outside Bangladesh. We choose providers that protect it with industry-standard security.

6. Cookies

We use only the cookies the shop needs to work. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Cookies we use

7. How long we keep it

  • Account details: until you delete your account.
  • Orders: as long as Bangladeshi tax and accounting law requires, even after an account is deleted, because they are business records.
  • Carts and holds: holds end after 15 minutes. The cookie that links a browser to its cart before sign-in expires after 30 days.
  • Server logs: kept only for a short period for security.

8. Security

The site uses HTTPS everywhere. Passwords and sign-in tokens are stored only as secure hashes, and sign-in sessions expire. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use elsewhere.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account. Email {{ shop.contactEmail }} from the address on your account and we'll reply within 30 days. If you signed in with Google, you can also remove our access from your Google account at any time.

10. Children

This shop is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes and contact

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page. Questions or requests: {{ shop.contactEmail }}. See also our terms and conditions.
